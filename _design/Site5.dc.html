Already using a timer app? Keep it. Missing Hours covers the hours you forgot to start it for.

No account. No cloud. It just runs along.

Everything stays on your PC. Recordings delete themselves after 30 days, or after the period you choose.

  • Just the app and window title in front of you. No screenshots, no keystrokes.
  • The only connection: an update check on GitHub.
  • Free and open source. Read the code on GitHub [LICENSE]

Running a team? Missing Hours has no team view. The hours people report come from a record of their day.
