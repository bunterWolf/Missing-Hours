Privacy

Last updated: 25 November 2026

The app

Missing Hours keeps everything it records on your computer and sends none of it anywhere. What it records, where it stays and for how long is described in the privacy policy of the app.

The app sends nothing about your day. It goes online for two things: to check for updates (on GitHub if you installed it from this website, or through the Microsoft Store if you installed it from there), and to load its font from Google Fonts when it starts.

Download button

So that the download button always offers the newest version, your browser asks GitHub (api.github.com) for the latest release when you open the start page. The installer itself is downloaded from GitHub as well. Both requests send GitHub the same technical data as above; the legal basis is the same.

No cookies, no tracking

This website sets no cookies and uses no analytics, tracking or advertising. The fonts are served from this website, not from a third party.
